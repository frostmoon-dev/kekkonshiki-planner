## 1. Malay wedding: what is needed

### Adat (traditional) steps

1. **Merisik**: an older relative of the groom asks the bride's family if she is free to marry.
2. **Meminang**: the formal proposal. Families agree on wang hantaran, mas kahwin and dates.
3. **Bertunang**: engagement. Ring and gift trays are exchanged.
4. **Berinai**: henna night, a few days before the nikah.
5. **Akad nikah**: the marriage contract (ijab kabul) before the wali and witnesses.
6. **Bersanding**: the reception on the pelamin (wedding dais).

Source: The Knot.

### Paperwork (Muslim couples)

- The **pre-marriage course** (kursus pra-perkahwinan) is required before you can apply for permission to marry. Register on the SPPIM portal or the MySPPIM app. (MyGOV, JAIN Johor guide, Nov 2025)
- **HIV screening** results are uploaded as part of the application in states that require it. The rules started in Johor in 2001. Other states followed.
- ⚠️ VERIFY: fees, the documents list and timing differ by state. Ask your state religious department (JAIN) or check SPPIM.

## 3. Colour planning

Popular theme colours in Malaysia (SAYS, 2025): baby blue, royal yellow (kuning diraja), mint green, light brown (coklat cair), light purple (ungu cair), grey, maroon.

- Grey is rare for the pelamin because it reads dark.
- Maroon works best paired with other colours.
- Soft tones such as champagne, peach and soft grey are popular in bridal jewellery.

The planner turns these into 8 palettes with hex codes and a split for akad nikah, bersanding, pelamin and the groom's samping. The split is my suggestion, not from a source.

## 4. Weight loss

- **BMI cut-offs (Malaysia, Asian)**: overweight 23–27.4, obese 27.5 and above. From Malaysia's 2023 obesity CPG, as quoted by secondary sources.
- **Safe pace**: 0.5–1 kg per week. About a 600 kcal daily deficit (NHS / NICE guidance, as summarised by third-party sites).
- **Calorie floor**: the planner does not go below 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men). This is a common rule of thumb, not from the sources above.
- **Plate**: Malaysia's Suku Suku Separuh: ¼ rice, ¼ protein, ½ vegetables and fruit.
- **Planner rule**: lose until 4 weeks before the wedding, then hold. Final fitting happens at the start of the hold.

This is not medical advice. Check the plan with a doctor or dietitian, especially with any health condition.
